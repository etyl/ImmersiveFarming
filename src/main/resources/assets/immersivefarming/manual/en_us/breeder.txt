Animal Breeder
Two stalls and a trough

<&multiblock>
The Animal Breeder breeds two animals without anyone feeding them by hand.

It has two stalls side by side, each behind a gate at the back. An open gate takes in the adult animal standing in front of it, and closes behind it. Once a stall is taken, the other only takes an animal that can breed with the first one.

The trough at the front is shared by both stalls. It is filled through the input above it, at the top of the machine: by a hopper, a conveyor, or by dropping food on it. It can also be filled by hand, as a Feeding Trough. When both animals are ready to breed and the trough holds <config;i;immersivefarming.breeder_food> items of a food they eat, they eat, and <config;i;immersivefarming.breeder_time> seconds later the newborn comes out of the hatch between the two gates. The parents then rest as after any breeding, and start again as long as there is food.<config;b;immersivefarming.babies_need_food; The newborns still need to be fed to grow up: keep a Feeding Trough in their pen.;>

Right click the back of the machine to open or close the gate on that side: opening a gate lets its animal out, and a gate closed on an empty stall keeps animals out.
