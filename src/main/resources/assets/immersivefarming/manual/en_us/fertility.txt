Soil Fertility
A nice soil

Crops on unfertilized soil only grow <config;i;immersivefarming.unfertilized_growth_percent>% as fast as on fertilized soil.

You can boost the fertility of a soil tile by right clicking it with compost.
Once a crop is fully grown on a fertilized tile, it will consume the fertilization, and the tile will need to be fertilized again.

<config;b;immersivefarming.require_sunlight;Crops also need sunlight: a crop that cannot see the sky will not grow. Glass lets sunlight through, so greenhouses work, but torches and lamps are not enough.;Crops do not need sunlight: any light source is enough, as in vanilla.>

Soil can be turned back into dirt by right clicking it with a shovel.

<&trowel>
The trowel lets you inspect soil. Right click soil or a crop to see how far it is tilled, its water level, its fertility and whether it is contaminated.
